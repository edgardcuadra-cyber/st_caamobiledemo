CAA Travel Insurance

Going to the United States? Cover the trip before you leave.

Provincial health plans pay only a small part of hospital and doctor bills in the US. Members in good standing save 10% on the premium.

Recommended for US trips

Emergency Medical

Up to $10 million CAD for covered emergency costs, such as hospital stays, doctor visits and ambulance services after an unexpected illness or injury.

Single trip, or multi-trip with trips of 4, 8, 15, 30 or 60 days each, up to 365 days a year. A higher deductible lowers the premium.

Families with bookings

Vacation Package

Emergency medical coverage plus trip protection in one policy, for families who have prepaid hotels, theme park tickets or other bookings.

Single trip, or multi-trip with trips of 4, 8, 15 or 30 days each.

Trip costs only

Trip Cancellation and Interruption

Protects prepaid trip costs if a covered reason forces you to cancel before leaving or come home early. It doesn't include emergency medical coverage.
